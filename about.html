how I work

The model is the hands. Not the head.

"Built with AI" usually conjures a prompt and a shrug. That isn't the job. The job is judgement — knowing what to build, breaking it into pieces a tool can nail, and being able to defend what comes back. The method should be invisible in the result.

01

Architect first

The shape of the system exists before any code does. Layering rules, security boundaries, public surface — those are decisions I own, not ones a model stumbles into.

02

Decompose into streams

A large system isn't one conversation. It's hundreds of small, well-scoped tasks with clear inputs, clear outputs, and a test to point at. The decomposition is the skill.

03

Direct the tools

I build primarily with Claude Code, treating it as a fast, literal, tireless pair of hands — pointed at one focused stream at a time and held to the architecture I set.

04

Review everything

Fast and tireless and occasionally confidently wrong. The only defence against the last one is a developer who understands exactly what came back — so every output is reviewed before it ships.
